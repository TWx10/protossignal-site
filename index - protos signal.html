Protos Signal

New York City bars and restaurants, found before they open.

The best time to sell to a new restaurant is before it opens, while the owner is still choosing vendors. After opening day, those decisions are made and you are trying to replace someone.

Protos Signal is a weekly list of New York City bars and restaurants that are on their way to opening, compiled from public records and screened so that only new venues and new operators reach you.

What subscribers receive

  • New venues and new operatorsRenewals and minor changes at places already open are filtered out.
  • Enough to make the callTrade name once published, operating company, street address, neighborhood, concept and license type.
  • Where it standsUpcoming public hearing date and licensing status, updated as each venue moves forward.
  • Every TuesdayBy email, as a spreadsheet and a short summary. Status carries forward week to week.

Who it is for

Any business that wins a new restaurant's account before opening day and keeps it for years.

  • Food and beverage supplyWine, spirits and beer distributors, food distributors, specialty and coffee suppliers.
  • Equipment and build-outKitchen and bar equipment, refrigeration and ice machines, draft systems, furniture, fixtures and signage.
  • Facility servicesPest control, hood and exhaust cleaning, fire suppression, grease trap service, waste and recycling, commercial cleaning.
  • Recurring suppliesLinen, uniforms, tabletop and glassware, paper and disposables.
  • Technology and paymentsPoint-of-sale, reservations and ordering, payments, payroll and scheduling software.
  • Professional servicesInsurance brokers, accountants, staffing and recruiting, marketing and design studios.
